# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rule is short: be decent to the people you work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the change, not the person.
- Keep feedback specific and kind. A review comment says what to change and why.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain jargon and answer questions without making anyone feel stupid.
- Respect other people's time: check whether an issue exists before opening one, and say when you
  can't keep working on something you claimed.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or content that has nothing to do with the project.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block someone who keeps breaking them. This applies to this repository and to the project's
support server.

## Reporting

Tell a maintainer privately: message one in the [support server](https://discord.gg/nMJJ8PAcD9), or
use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment. Reports are kept private, and the maintainer who
receives one decides what to do about it; someone involved in a report doesn't decide it.

This code of conduct is short on purpose. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, which you
can read in full at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org>.
