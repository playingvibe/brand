# Vibe: brand sheet

One page. The files themselves are in [`brand/vectors/`](brand/vectors/); everything else is drawn from them.

## The mark

The V is three circles joined by tangent bars, with a rounded inner corner. It is one shape (`brand/vectors/v-mark.svg`),
and the word (`brand/vectors/vibe-wordmark.svg`) is built the same way, so the two sit together without a seam.

- Fill it with one flat colour. No gradient, outline, shadow, glow or second colour inside it.
- Keep clear space of half the V's width on every side.
- Smallest size: 16 px tall as a bare mark, 22 px as a tile.
- Put it on the near-black ground, on a bot's colour, or on white. Never on a photo or a busy pattern.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour outside the palette below, add a drop shadow or draw it in outline only.

| Vibe 2 | `#1A79FF` | the second bot |
| Vibe 3 | `#FFC20A` | the third bot (its V is white) |
| Vibe 4 | `#9B29FF` | the fourth bot |
| Vibe Beta | `#00A331` | the beta bot |
| Vibe Dev | `#0B0B0D` | the development bot, with a ring and a grey V |

Colours are flat. A bot is its colour plus the white V; nothing else tells the family apart.

## Type

[Outfit](fonts/OFL-Outfit.txt) (Regular, Medium, Bold) for everything; [Sacramento](fonts/OFL-Sacramento.txt) only for
signatures. Both are under the SIL Open Font License.

## Avatars and emoji

- Bot avatars: the V on the bot's colour, a square image; Discord rounds it.
- Avatar emoji: the same tile as a rounded square, corner radius 28.7 % of the side, on transparency.
- Marks: the bare V in the bot's colour on transparency.

## Badges

The glyph says the track and the container says the tier.

| Track | Glyph |
|---|---|
| time listened | the V |
| records | stacked tracks |
| streak | a flame, larger for each tier |
| votes | an arrow |

Tiers, lowest to highest: no container, ring, disc, rhombus, hexagon. Founder is a star on the flagship colour; premium is a
crown on gold (`#BD8B00`); boost is a pink gem (`#FF73FA`).

## Banners and server art

Banners are the word in the bot's colour on the ground, one per bot. The server art and the Activity images follow the
same rule: flat colour, one V or one word, nothing else.
